Common questions

Do I need to log in to check delivery?

No. Enter your PIN code and the result shows straight away. Login is only needed when you are ready to pay.

What if a product cannot be delivered to my PIN code?

It is clearly marked and cannot be added to the cart, so you never end up with items you cannot receive.

What happens if I change my PIN code later?

Your cart is checked again. Anything that cannot be delivered to the new PIN is flagged before you continue.
